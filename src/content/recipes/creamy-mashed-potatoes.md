---
title: Creamy Mashed Potatoes
image: creamy-mashed-potatoes.jpg
tags: Side Dish
category: Side Dish
yield: 10 Servings
preptime: 1 Hour
cooktime: 30 Minutes
 
ingredients:
- 5 Pounds Russet or Yukon Gold Potatoes
- 3/4 Cup Butter, softened, plus more for topping
- 1 (8 Ounce) Package Cream Cheese, softened
- 1/2 Cup Half-and-Half
- 1/4 Cup Heavy Cream
- 1/2 to 1 teaspoon Lawry's Seasoned Salt
- 2 teaspoons Black Pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon Kosher Salt, plus more to taste
 
directions:
- Peel the potatoes and cut them into evenly sized chunks.
- Add potatoes to a large pot of simmering water. Bring to a boil and cook 30 to 35 minutes, until a fork slides in with no resistance and the potatoes are nearly falling apart.
- Drain well in a colander. Return potatoes to the dry pot over low heat and mash for about 3 minutes to let the steam escape.
- Turn off the heat. Add butter, cream cheese, half-and-half, and heavy cream and mash until smooth.
- Season with seasoned salt, pepper, and kosher salt. Taste and adjust seasoning.
- Spread into a buttered medium baking dish and dot the top with pats of butter.
- Bake at 350 Degrees until the butter melts and the potatoes are heated through.
 
notes:
- Mash by hand with a potato masher. A mixer or food processor can make the potatoes gummy.
- To make ahead, cover with foil and refrigerate up to 2 days. Take out 2 to 3 hours before serving, then bake covered at 350 Degrees for 20 to 30 minutes until warmed through.
- Leftovers keep up to 4 days in the fridge, or freeze up to 1 month.
